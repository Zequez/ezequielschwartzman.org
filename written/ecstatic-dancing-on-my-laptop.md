# Ecstatic Dancing On My Laptop

----------

So as you probably have found, there is music that it can become
pleasurable to listen to.

Have you ever tried sitting on your computer to do some creative work,
like you would create the dance of your life on a dome room? If you add
music it becomes the soundtrack of your life. You see yourself working
through the eyes of an expectator of a very cool character, doing some
cool shit, and it's all very exciting; every keystroke feels of massive
importance and things shift moment to moment.

Very entertaining.

Now, as for where I was going.

Maybe it's all poetry, and I can write and leave things incomplete.

Or maybe it's all improvization, and  I can change my mind at any time about anything and do a 90 degree shift at the speed of light. Zipping around as I process information so fast that other people miss it. Also a cool character.

Maybe it's both, and everything that flows from me it's my very essence, my creative juice energy reaching the hearts and souls of an amorphous blob of blurry beings all mixed and mashed together in an orgy of passionate lovemaking.

---------

And so it goes on. I lose myself in a thought, and then I hear the music again, and I dive into the rhythm, and my body starts vibrating slowly, and so I feel my body, I feel alive, and movements are more and more fluid and alive, and there is this energy running wild inside me.

And then the song ends, and a new one begins. And I vibrate differently, and so it's fast, it's refreshing, it's a little scary, I feel afraid my vibrating will disturb people around me, I'll be looked at, I can't be invisible then, I will be sad, I'm afraid I'll be sad if I enjoy myself too much.

And so that thought and emotion ended. And the music goes on, and I already left it.

# What if

What if I could just write on this one document, and everything I wrote would get published and sorted automatically by some machine that would organize it in a way that the things I write are the most valuable for all.

I don't need to care about what I wrote before this one letter. So light, every sentence with the full potential of possibility. Unbound by the previously written. Unburdened by expectations or unresolved tension. Maybe tension can be left unresolved. How titillating. How very heretic.

And so, I can leave every pharagrph as poetry and not care if what I write makes sense right now or did at some point. If you read it and it touches something in you, then what does it matter if I'm eating ice cream right now? It does not, and so what I do after I write is of no consequence to the truth of what is written.

I can always do-over something written to polish it, rather than attempt to fix it, like it's imperfect.

What I write is perfect, rather than trying to fix it, I can strenghten my writing hand and by repeated effort, write in such a way that a more genuine and authentic energy flows from my movement.

And so I can write with confidence, with lightness, with ecstatic bliss of creation. And then  there is the music.
